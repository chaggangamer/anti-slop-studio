# Edit, QA and delivery

Contents: review passes, keep/repair/replace/rebuild, campaign sameness, delivery checklist, final report.

## Review passes

Watch the piece at more than one speed and size. Each pass catches something different.

1. **Full size, sound on:** continuity, artefacts, pacing, mix.
2. **Sound off:** does the story land from picture and captions alone?
3. **Eyes closed:** does the audio carry rhythm and meaning?
4. **Thumbnail size:** is there a focal point and legible text?
5. **Frame by frame at cuts:** garbled text, drifting faces, stray watermarks, usernames, platform UI.
6. **Second watch:** is anything new on the second viewing?
7. **Alternate crops:** vertical, square and horizontal versions each have intentional framing, not automatic crops that lose the subject.

## Keep, repair, replace, rebuild

Classify each scene or section.

| Decision | When | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Keep | Supports the message and meets the bar | Finish normally |
| Repair | Core image works with fixable issues | Crop, stabilise, retime, composite, grade, replace text, fix sound |
| Replace | Distracts, drifts, or cannot show the needed evidence | Regenerate, or use stock, screen capture, animation or live action |
| Rebuild | The script, structure or concept is the problem | Re-plan before making more assets |

A shorter edit with fewer controlled shots usually feels more professional than a long reel of impressive but unrelated generations.

## Campaign sameness check

When making several pieces, build a matrix and compare them to each other, not only to the brief.

| Piece | Story | Visual world | Pacing | Soundtrack | Outro | One-liner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

If two rows match in more than one column, change the weaker piece. Variation of concept comes first, then of visual world; a shared typography system or end card is allowed when it is a deliberate brand decision.

## Technical QA

- Resolution and aspect ratio match the target (for example 1080x1920 for vertical).
- No visible watermarks, usernames, platform chrome or placeholder text.
- Audio audible and within the loudness and peak targets; no clipping; no dead air at the head or tail unless intended.
- Captions: correct text, correct spelling, readable duration, inside safe areas; deliver an SRT where useful.
- Fonts, logos and contact details are the real assets, copied exactly from approved material.
- Colour and texture consistent across shots (exposure, temperature, saturation, grain).
- File opens and plays on a phone, not only in the editor.

## Delivery checklist

- Final file named clearly, with version.
- Script, caption text, shot list and QA notes alongside the video when requested.
- Source and licence record for music, fonts, stock and any generated assets.
- Nothing published, uploaded, deployed or spent without the user's say-so.

## Final report (keep it short)

1. What was made and the one idea behind it.
2. Which checks ran and their result.
3. What is still weak or unverified (for example native-speaker review, rights clearance).
4. Any deliberate exceptions to the defaults and why.

Do not claim that a clean scan proves quality, that a piece is "best ever", or that something was verified when it was not.
